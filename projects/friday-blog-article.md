# Friday Blog Article — Standing Procedure

This file documents Kyle Friedman's repeatable Friday content piece
workflow: a buyer- or seller-focused deep-dive article, distributed to
Substack and the website, with supporting image prompts and a video script.
When Kyle asks for "the Friday article" (or similar), follow this procedure
end-to-end without re-asking for the process.

## What "the Friday article" is, every time — 3 deliverables
1. **A written article** (buyer- or seller-focused educational deep-dive)
   for Substack and the website SEO article
2. **4 detailed image prompts** for use in ChatGPT/DALL-E/Midjourney
3. **A 15–20 second social video script** — story/hook-driven, NOT Kyle
   reciting data to camera. Needs a hook, a turn/twist, and a short payoff.
   Format as a time-coded table (time / visual+edit direction / audio or
   on-screen text).

## Step-by-step process
1. **Check `friday-blog-article-log.md` first** — read every topic already
   covered. Never repeat a topic. Pick something new.
2. **Pick a topic** — buyer- or seller-focused, Maryland/Carroll-County
   relevant where possible. Favor topics that reinforce brand positioning
   (see `../notes/brand-guidelines.md`) — e.g. the farm/equestrian niche —
   over generic national real estate content.
3. **Research the topic** before writing — use web search for anything
   involving specific laws, tax rates, program names, or numbers. Don't
   state Maryland-specific legal/tax/financing facts from memory alone;
   verify. Hedge appropriately where sources are general/national rather
   than Maryland-specific (e.g. USDA loan nuances) rather than overstating
   confidence.
4. **Write the article** — match the length, tone, and educational-deep-dive
   style of prior pieces (see log for topic list; roughly 800–1200 words,
   H2-structured, ends with a Kyle/Friedman Team CTA). Deliver as a file
   suitable for both Substack and a website SEO post.
5. **Write 4 image prompts** — detailed scene/prop/composition prompts
   matching brand colors (deep teal `#0F5C63`, warm gold `#C9A96A`).
   - Do NOT attempt to generate Kyle's actual face via AI image tools —
     text-to-image tools can't reliably reproduce a specific real person,
     and this isn't something to do even if it could. If Kyle wants
     himself in a graphic, the prompts should note: use a real photo of
     Kyle + add text/graphics in Canva or Adobe Express, not AI generation.
   - Don't rely on AI tools to render legible on-image text — recommend
     adding text as a post-process step (Canva) rather than baking it into
     the image prompt.
6. **Write the video script** — hook in the first 2-3 seconds, a turn/twist
   around the midpoint, short payoff/CTA at the end. Table format: time,
   visual/edit direction, audio or on-screen text. No long talking-head
   segments.
7. **Log the new topic** — append it to `friday-blog-article-log.md` so
   future runs never repeat it.

## Distribution
Same standard channels as Friedman Report topic pieces: Substack + website
SEO article always; Instagram carousel is standard distribution per
`friedman-report.md`. Video script is for whichever short-form platform
Kyle is posting to that week (Instagram Reels/TikTok/YouTube Shorts).
