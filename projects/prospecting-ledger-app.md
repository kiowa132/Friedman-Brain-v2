# Prospecting Ledger App

## What it is
A simple daily prospecting performance tracker web app (not a CRM) — tracks
cold-calling numbers, kept separate from Follow Up Boss (CRM) and Mojo
(dialer).

## Decisions
- Deploy on a free Netlify subdomain, not a custom domain.
- No login — a private, hard-to-guess URL instead of a password.

## Status
Deployed and hosted on a real, shareable URL.
