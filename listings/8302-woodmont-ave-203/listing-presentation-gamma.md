## Why eight price cuts kill a listing

When buyers watch a price drop again and again, three things happen:

- They stop making offers and wait for the next cut.
- They assume something is wrong with the unit.
- After 180-plus days the listing goes stale and drops out of their saved searches.

By the time the price was finally reasonable, the listing was effectively invisible. The fix is not another cut. It is a clean relaunch at one correct price.

## The plan: a relaunch, not a re-list

**How the days-on-market reset works.** Bright MLS tracks Days on Market and Cumulative Days on Market. The cumulative counter only resets to zero after a property has been off the market for 90 consecutive days. Zillow works the same way: "days on Zillow" resets once the home has been off Zillow about 90 days; relist any sooner and Zillow keeps the old day count, shows the full price-cut history, and flags it as "relisted." Your listing came off on August 21, so if it stays off, both counters are clean around mid-to-late November.

From there, two launch windows: an early-December launch into the winter relocation and year-end buyer pool, or hold to mid-January for the larger spring-adjacent pool. We pick based on where the competing inventory sits.
