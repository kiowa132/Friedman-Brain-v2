**What it is**

Zillow Showcase is Zillow's premium listing format. It is not on every listing, only through select agents, and it replaces Zillow's standard photo-grid template with an immersive, interactive presentation built for the home.

**1. AI-powered virtual staging**

Instead of one AI-generated furniture look, the way the earlier listing had it, Showcase lets a buyer tap into the home and choose from curated design styles: modern, Scandinavian, industrial, midcentury, luxury, coastal, or farmhouse. The AI restyles the room to match, whether it is empty or already furnished, and a buyer can refresh the furniture arrangement within the same style or clear it back to a blank slate. Every buyer sees the home in the style that speaks to them, not one generic look.
Read more: https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2025-09-10-Zillow-brings-AI-powered-Virtual-Staging-to-Showcase-listings

**2. Interactive floor plan**

Not a static blueprint. It is AI-built and combines the layout with the actual design of the home, so a buyer can move through it and immediately understand how the main-level primary and the finished lower level connect.

**3. SkyTour**

A drone-like, interactive exterior tour. Buyers explore the outside of the home and the surrounding block from multiple angles and elevations, right from their phone or computer, without ever leaving the listing.
Read more: https://www.zillow.com/news/skytour-is-pushing-the-tech-boundaries-of-real-estate-visualization/

**4. Priority placement**

Showcase listings sit above standard listings in Zillow's search results and on the map view. That is exposure the earlier listing did not have.

**5. Immersive design**

High-resolution photography and an interactive layout built specifically for the listing, not the generic template every other home on Zillow uses.

**The numbers**

Zillow's own data on Showcase listings:

- 75 percent more page views, saves, and shares than similar nearby listings
- 20 percent more likely to get an accepted offer within 14 days
- Sell for about $7,000 more on average than similar non-Showcase listings

More time on a listing means more buyers picture themselves living there, and that is what turns a scroll into a showing and a showing into an offer.
