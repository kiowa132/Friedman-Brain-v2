# Canceling with Mari — Nikki Fern, 7721 Wilson Ave

Simple version: how to tell Nikki what canceling with her current agent
actually takes.

---

"So here's how canceling works. Coming off the MLS didn't end your
agreement, it just took it off the market. Your actual contract runs
through December 30th. But you have two easy ways out of it:

One, you just ask Mari for a mutual release, most agents will let you go
without a fight once you tell them you're moving on.

Two, if she doesn't, you can send her written notice and it ends
automatically in 10 days. You don't need her to agree to that one.

Either way, get it in writing, even if she says 'yeah, that's fine' on the
phone, ask for one quick email confirming it. That's what actually makes it
official."

---

## Email if she agrees

> Subject: 7721 Wilson Ave, Confirming Release from Listing Agreement
>
> Hi Mari,
>
> I'd like to confirm in writing that we've mutually agreed to terminate the
> listing agreement for 7721 Wilson Ave, effective [date].
>
> Thank you for everything up to this point.
>
> Nikki

> Subject: 7721 Wilson Ave, Notice of Termination

>
> This is my written notice of termination of our listing agreement dated
> 5/14/26, effective 10 days from today.
>
> Thank you,
> Nikki Fern
