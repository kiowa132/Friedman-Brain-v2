Good talking today. Here is the full plan, built backward from a relaunch in early October, plus how to handle the basement now that Allstate is not sending an adjuster.

**What "no adjuster" changes.**
It is good news. It usually means Allstate will settle the claim off ECO Clean's written scope, the photos, and the moisture logs, without an inspection holding you up. Two things to lock in before you spend any real money:

1. Get it in writing from Allstate: what cause of loss they are covering, your deductible on this claim, and whether they pay ECO Clean directly or reimburse you.
2. Have ECO Clean send Allstate a written scope with moisture readings and photos. That document is the whole claim now.

Once that is confirmed, you are clear to move fast, because nothing is waiting on an inspection.

**The one non-negotiable: fix the cause.**
New drywall over an unsolved water problem fails a buyer's inspection every time. Whatever ECO Clean and the contractor find, groundwater, a plumbing failure, water in through the walk-up, the fix includes solving the source. A sump pump, a regrade, a drain at the walk-up, whatever it calls for. That is the part I would not DIY. Everything else you can.

**On hanging the drywall yourself.**
Fine, and it saves money. Two rules: wait for ECO Clean to confirm the framing is fully dry before you close anything up, and know that if Allstate is paying a rebuild allowance, doing the work yourself means you keep that labor money.
