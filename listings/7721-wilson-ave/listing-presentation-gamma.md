## The number one problem: your home was listed as a 1,300 square foot house

On the MLS, 7721 Wilson was entered at about 1,287 square feet above grade. With the finished lower level, the home is closer to 2,100 finished square feet, and that is what Zillow and Redfin show. That one number did two things.

---

## What the wrong square footage did

**It made the home invisible in search.** Buyers filter by size. Anyone searching for 1,400, 1,500, or 1,600 square feet and up never saw the listing.

**It made the price look high.**

| Square footage used | Math at $324,900 | How it reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1,287 sf, as listed | $252 per sf | Expensive, priced above the comps |
| 2,145 sf, actual finished | $151 per sf | A strong value, priced to move |

Same house, same price, opposite impression.

---

## Two more things that cost you offers

**The launch.** The first week of a new listing is roughly 80% of the buyer traffic it will ever get, and you only get that week once. Photos and staging have to be right before it goes live, not patched in during week two.

**Two price cuts in 25 days.** When buyers watch a price fall twice in three weeks, they stop making offers and wait for the next cut. The listing goes stale and stalls. One correct price held with a plan beats a ladder of cuts.

The good news: none of this is about your home or your street.

---

## How a home actually sells

Every sale runs through the same four steps. A listing stalls when it loses one.

| Step | What it does | What happened here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Get Found | Show up in the searches buyers run: full square footage, right price band, complete data, syndicated everywhere | Lost. Listed at 1,287 sf, so it fell out of every search with a size filter |
| 2. Get the Click | The first photo, price, and headline earn the tap. Zillow Showcase places it above standard listings | Weak. Photos re-ordered and AI staging added in week two, no Showcase, already stale |
| 3. Get the Showing | The online tour makes a buyer need to see it: floor plan, 3D tour, virtual staging, video | Partial. No floor plan, so the main-level primary and finished lower level did not read |
| 4. Get the Offer | Read the feedback, hold one correct price with a plan, negotiate | Lost. Two cuts in 25 days trained buyers to wait, then it was withdrawn |
