One more factor working against us: Montpelier Village is not FHA approved, so we
cannot take FHA buyers, who are most of the entry level pool for a condo at this
price. We are working with cash, conventional, and VA buyers only. That smaller
pool makes pricing right even more important, because we cannot count on buyer
volume to carry a high price.

MARKETING IT AS VACANT

Right now the unit is tenant occupied, the rent is below market, and there is a
lease in place. That limits us to investor buyers, and the investor math at this
price is weak because the rent is low. Owner occupant buyers, who usually pay
more, cannot buy it at all because they cannot get possession.

If you are open to it, I would like to market the home as vacant and deliver it
vacant at closing. Assuming we can arrange for the tenant to be out by closing,
whether by timing it to the lease or a cash for keys agreement, delivering it
vacant would:

- Open the unit to owner occupant buyers, a bigger and higher paying pool
- Make showings easy, with no restricted hours or coordination
- Let us take fresh photos of a clean, empty unit
- Remove the below market rent problem for investor buyers too

Let me know if that works for you and I will start the conversation with the
tenant.
