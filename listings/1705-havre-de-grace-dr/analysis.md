## Door offer vs. open market (the core pitch)

| | Sell to a door buyer | List on the open market |
|---|---|---|
| Who they are | Mostly investors / flippers / wholesalers who knock during renos looking for a discount and an unrepresented seller | Everyone — including families pre-approved and ready |
| Number of buyers | One at a time, take it or leave it | The whole market; multiple offers likely at $479,900 |
| Who picks the buyer | The buyer (you have no leverage) | **You do** — prioritize an owner-occupant family |
| Likely price | ~$420,000–$445,000 (near as-is value) | ~$470,000–$480,000 |
| Commission | $0 | ~5% (about $23,500) |
| Net before mortgage payoff (est.) | ~$430,000 | **~$445,000–$450,000** |
| Time | Fast but you leave money on the table | ~2–4 weeks to contract at this price in this market |
| Risk / work | You handle disclosures, contract, financing, inspections yourself | Handled, with a transaction coordinator |

**Even after commission, listing nets ~$15,000–$25,000 more than a door offer,
and MORE vs. a lowball ($40K+).** And the family buyer she wants is far more
likely on the MLS than knocking mid-reno.
