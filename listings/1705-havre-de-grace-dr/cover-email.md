**Subject:** The buyers at your door, and what listing would actually get you

Hi Jefferson,

Good catching up. You mentioned that while you renovate, buyers keep coming to
the door asking to buy, and that you would rather sell to a family than to an
investor. I pulled the numbers on 1705 Havre De Grace and put together a straight
breakdown of your options. It is attached, along with a sheet comparing what you
net from a door sale versus listing.

Here is the short version.

The buyers knocking are a good sign, but they are almost all investors, flippers,
and wholesalers. They watch for renovation permits and dumpsters, and they are
betting on an unrepresented seller who wants a quick, quiet sale. Their offer is
built to capture the value your renovation is creating, for themselves. The
family buyer who would love this house is not walking your street mid renovation.
They are on Zillow, already approved with a lender, waiting for it to come on the
market finished.

The market backs that up. An unrenovated 3 bedroom this size in Woodland Beach
sells around $415,000 to $430,000. 1622 Chesapeake Dr, the same 1,668 square
feet, sold at $430,000 in original condition. Finished and on the open market,
the same size sells at $470,000 to $480,000. 1604 Oriole Rd, a renovated home
almost exactly your size, sold at $480,000 in 13 days. That is a roughly $50,000
spread, and it is what a door buyer wants to keep.

So even after a full commission, listing the finished home nets you about $15,000
to $25,000 more than a door offer at original condition value, and more than that
against a lowball. The details are in the attached comparison. Your mortgage
payoff comes off the top either way, so it does not change which path nets more.

On the goal of selling to a family, listing is how you actually get there. On the
open market you control who buys it. We market to and prioritize owner occupant
buyers, read their letters, and put a no assignment clause and a primary
residence occupancy affidavit in the contract so wholesalers and flippers are
screened out. If a family's offer comes in a little under an investor's, that is
your call to make. With one buyer at your door, you get one number and no choice.

Professional photography of the finished renovation plus a twilight exterior. A
