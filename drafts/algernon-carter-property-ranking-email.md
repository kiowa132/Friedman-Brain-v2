1. 35 Glenwood Ave, Catonsville, $330,000

Heads up, I have a listing appointment Saturday at 2 PM, so if you want me there for this open house we need to figure out timing, or you walk it first and tell me what you think.

2. 3706 Parkfield Rd, Pikesville, $360,000

Got better information on this one and it moved way up the list. The basement was finished in 2024, not someday, already done, and it's a real 4th bedroom plus a family room and a full bath with a walk in shower. Condition is excellent, kitchen was redone with stainless appliances, all the appliances are two years old or newer, roof is five years old. It sits on a landscaped cul de sac lot. The seller is also offering a 2 percent credit toward your closing costs, which goes straight at what you want, keeping cash in your pocket. The one real gap, parking is only 2 spaces on the driveway, not the 4 you are looking for, so we would need to figure out if street parking nearby covers the rest.

3. 3655 Forest Garden Ave, Baltimore County, $359,999

Real contender. Level backyard, and the lower level is already finished with its own full bath and a kitchenette, it was used as an in-law suite, so turning it into a 4th bedroom is closer to done than a project. Right around 2,000 square feet. It's been on and off the market for over a year and cut 17 percent from its original ask, which tells me there's real room to negotiate below the current price, not just accept it at $359,999. I want to ask the listing agent directly why it hasn't sold yet before we tour it. Parkfield beats it on condition and comes with that seller credit, but this one is right behind it.

4. 5412 W North Ave, Gwynn Oak, $335,000

Good bones, $25,000 under your approval. It already has a fenced yard and a big basement, over 1,000 square feet, but that basement is unfinished, so getting a usable 4th bedroom or bathroom down there means real renovation money, which cuts against wanting to keep cash free for that. Finished living space right now is under your size target too. Worth a look, but knowing the reno isn't optional if you want the basement to count for anything.

5. 2909 Louisiana Ave, Halethorpe, $369,900

On paper this checks the most boxes, the basement is already finished into a legit 4th bedroom with its own bath. But it's $9,900 over your approval, which matters given you want to keep cash to close low, and it's in Halethorpe, near BWI, a different area than everywhere else on this list. Bigger flag, this house has been listed and pulled four separate times in under a year with $30,000 in cuts despite being marketed as fully renovated. That pattern usually means something came up in an appraisal or inspection before. I would not put an offer in without asking the listing agent point blank why it keeps coming back on the market.

6. 7165 Fairbrook Rd, Windsor Mill, $325,000

Cheapest and already updated throughout, but it fails two of your must haves outright. No basement at all, so there is nowhere to add that 4th bedroom, and it's 1,504 square feet, well under what you want. Good price does not make up for the house not being able to do what you need it to do.

My take: tour 35 Glenwood and 3706 Parkfield first. Glenwood wins on parking and price cushion, Parkfield wins on condition and comes with that seller credit, its only real miss is parking. Forest Garden is right behind both. Let me know if you want me at that open house Saturday and we will sort out the timing.
