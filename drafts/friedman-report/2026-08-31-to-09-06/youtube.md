**Description:**
This week's Friedman Report opens with a correction: last week's report
showed Maryland closings and new listings "nearly doubling," and that was
a data-coverage problem, not a real market move — two weeks ago the

to 21, and the Freddie Mac 30-year fixed up to 6.71%, its second straight
weekly increase. The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 50/100 —
Balanced Market, Holding Steady.

sold-to-original-list stat that says the softening is shallow, and what
it all means if you're buying or selling in Carroll, Baltimore, Howard,
Frederick, or Montgomery County this fall.
