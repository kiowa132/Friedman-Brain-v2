## Long Version (~1,400 characters)

Maryland's housing market is holding steady.

Volume held its level this week. Closings were near 1,150, in line with
the week before, and new listings were flat at 2,016. Pending contracts
rose 5.7% to 518. What actually moved was price and pace: the median sold
price fell 2.2% to $440,000, median days-to-contract slowed from 18 to
21, and Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed rose 5 basis points to 6.71%, its
second straight weekly increase. A mild autumn softening, not a turn.

The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 50 out of 100 — Balanced Market,
Holding Steady. Right in the middle of the scale, with the four
components close to offsetting.

Even with 2,098 price reductions statewide, the typical closed home still
sold for 99.0% of its original list price — the softening is real but
shallow, and it's concentrated in listings that mispriced at launch.
Harford County was the fastest market in the state (25-day average, 55
closings). Carroll County sellers still sold above their original list
price.
