## Caption

Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: steady. Volume held its
level — closings near 1,150, new listings flat at 2,016, pending up 5.7%
to 518. What actually moved was price and pace: median sold price down
2.2% to $440,000, days-to-contract from 18 to 21, and the 30-year rate up
to 6.71%, its second straight weekly increase. The Friedman Market
Momentum Index reads 50/100.

The number that reframes it: even with 2,098 price cuts, the typical
closed home still sold for 99.0% of its original list price. The
softening is real but shallow, and it's concentrated in listings that
mispriced at launch.

Harford County was the fastest market in the state. Carroll County
sellers still sold above their original list price.
