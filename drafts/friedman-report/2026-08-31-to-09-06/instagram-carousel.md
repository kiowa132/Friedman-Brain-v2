## Caption

Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: leveling. Last week's
surge didn't reverse — it just stopped accelerating. Closings held at
1,150, new listings flat at 2,016, pending up 5.7% to 518. Same elevated
volume, no further climb.

But every price and pace metric ticked the other way. Median sold price
fell 2.2% to $440,000, days-to-contract slowed from 18 to 21, and the
30-year rate rose to 6.71% — its second straight weekly increase. The
Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 52/100, down from 58, its first
decline in three weeks.

Harford County was the fastest market in the state (25-day average, 55
closings). Carroll County sellers still sold above their original list
price. And for the second week running, price reductions (2,098)
outnumbered new listings (2,016).

The read: a market leveling off, not turning. For a buyer with a rate
lock in hand, it's the friendliest week since spring. For a seller, it's
the week the market started grading your list price more strictly.
