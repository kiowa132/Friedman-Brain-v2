## Caption

Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: steady. But first, a
correction worth being upfront about — last week's report showed closings
and new listings "nearly doubling." That was a data-coverage gap on our
end catching up, not a real market move. Two weeks ago our Bright MLS
pull was missing several county feeds. This week is the first genuine
full-state week-over-week comparison in a month.

And it shows a market holding its level: closings flat at 1,150, new
listings flat at 2,016, pending up 5.7% to 518. What actually moved was
price and pace — median sold price down 2.2% to $440,000, days-to-contract
from 18 to 21, and the 30-year rate up to 6.71%, its second straight
weekly increase. The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 50/100.

The number that reframes it: even with 2,098 price cuts, the typical
closed home still sold for 99.0% of its original list price. The
softening is real but shallow, and it's concentrated in listings that
mispriced at launch.

Harford County was the fastest market in the state. Carroll County
sellers still sold above their original list price.
