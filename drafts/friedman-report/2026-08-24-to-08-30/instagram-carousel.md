## Caption

Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: surging. New listings
and closings both nearly doubled statewide — the sharpest simultaneous
supply-and-demand jump this report has tracked. The Friedman Market
Momentum Index reads 58/100, up modestly from 55, because Demand Score
spiked while Seller Strength actually softened as price reductions surged
right alongside the new inventory.

Carroll County held onto the fastest pace in Maryland for a second
straight week (19 days), and Montgomery County posted the highest closing
volume of any county (168). Median sold price kept climbing to $450,000,
and homes still closed at a median 99.3% of original list price — proof
that even with this much new supply, well-priced listings aren't getting
discounted.
