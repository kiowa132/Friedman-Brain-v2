Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: surging.

New listings jumped 89.8% statewide this week — the sharpest single-week
move this report has tracked, in either direction. Closings jumped almost
exactly as much, up 89.2%. Price reductions surged too, up 80.5%. Median
sold price kept climbing (+2.3%, to $450,000), and homes that closed sold
at a median 99.3% of their original asking price.

The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 58/100 this week — Balanced
Market, Surging Activity — up modestly from 55 last week. It only moved
+3 despite historic swings underneath because the four components pulled
in different directions: Demand Score jumped to 78 (from 62), but Seller
Strength Score actually fell to 50 (from 60) as reductions outpaced price
gains.

→ Carroll County: fastest pace in Maryland for a second straight week —
19 days average, and it got faster while adding volume
→ Montgomery County: highest closing volume of any county (168), still
moving at a healthy 28-day pace
→ Freddie Mac's 30-year fixed ticked up 1 bp to 6.66%, ending a two-week
decline streak

The read: this isn't a market tipping toward buyers or sellers — it's one
that got a lot busier, all at once, right at the seasonal turn into fall.
More choice for buyers, but more competition for the good listings too.
