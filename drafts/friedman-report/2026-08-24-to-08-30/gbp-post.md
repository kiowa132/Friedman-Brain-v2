## Long Version (~1,400 characters)

Maryland's housing market flooded back open this week — all at once.

New listings jumped 89.8% statewide, the sharpest single-week move this
report has tracked. Closings jumped almost exactly as much, up 89.2% to
1,137. Price reductions surged too, up 80.5%. Median sold price kept
climbing to $450,000 (+2.3%), and homes that closed sold at a median
99.3% of their original asking price — proof that even with this much new
supply landing at once, well-priced listings are still closing near full
ask.

The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 58 out of 100 this week —
Balanced Market, Surging Activity — up modestly from 55 last week. It
only moved a few points despite historic swings underneath, because Demand
Score spiked to 78 while Seller Strength Score actually softened to 50 as
reductions outpaced price gains.

Carroll County held the fastest pace in Maryland for a second straight
week (19-day average days on market), and Montgomery County posted the
highest closing volume of any county (168 homes). Freddie Mac's 30-year
fixed ticked up to 6.66%, ending a two-week decline streak.
