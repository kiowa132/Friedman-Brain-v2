**Title:** Maryland Real Estate Market Update: Week of September 7 – 13, 2026 | The Friedman Team

**Description:**
This week's Friedman Report: Maryland's housing market reset for fall.
New listings jumped 17.4% to 2,366, the biggest one-week supply surge
this report has tracked, while closings fell 38.4% to 708, mostly a
Labor Day settlement-calendar effect, not a demand break. Pending
contracts only slipped 7.1%. The surprise: sellers who closed this week
did better on price, not worse. Median sold price up 1.1% to $445,000,
and closed homes realized a median 99.6% of original list price, up from
99.0%. The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 48/100: Balanced
Market, Pricing Power Returns.

In this update: the full statewide numbers, a new county-by-county
supply-pressure breakdown showing where new listings are outpacing
closings (Prince George's, Baltimore City, and Montgomery lead), St.
Mary's County as the fastest market in the state, Garrett County as the
slowest, the 99.6% sold-to-original-list stat that says pricing power is
firming, and what it all means if you're buying or selling in Carroll,
Baltimore, Howard, Frederick, Montgomery, or Prince George's County this
fall.
