## Caption

Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: resetting. New listings
jumped 17.4% to 2,366, the biggest one-week supply surge this report has
tracked, sellers coming back from Labor Day all at once. Closings fell
38.4% to 708, mostly a holiday settlement-calendar effect, pending
contracts only slipped 7.1%. The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads
48/100.

The number that reframes it: even against the heaviest competition of
the year, closed homes sold for a median 99.6% of their ORIGINAL list
price, up from 99.0% the week before. Sellers gained ground, not lost it.

New this week: I broke out new listings against closings, county by
county. Prince George's, Baltimore City, and Montgomery are seeing new
supply build fastest, real room for buyers there. St. Mary's County was
the fastest-moving market in the state.
