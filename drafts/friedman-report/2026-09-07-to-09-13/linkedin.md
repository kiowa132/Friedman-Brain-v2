Maryland's housing market this week, in one word: resetting.

New listings jumped 17.4% to 2,366, the biggest one-week supply surge
this report has tracked, sellers coming back from Labor Day all at once,
asking a median 6.8% more money doing it. Closings fell 38.4% to 708,
mostly a holiday settlement-calendar effect, not a demand break: pending
contracts only slipped 7.1%.

Here's the part that should surprise you. Sellers who closed this week
did BETTER on price, not worse, even against the heaviest competition of
the year. Median sold price rose 1.1% to $445,000, and closed homes
realized a median 99.6% of their ORIGINAL list price, up from 99.0% the
week before.

The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 48/100, Balanced Market,
Pricing Power Returns. Demand 43, Seller Strength 56, Market Speed 60,
Rate Environment 32.

13 closings
