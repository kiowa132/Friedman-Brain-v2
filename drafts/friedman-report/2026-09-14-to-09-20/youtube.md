**Description:**
This week's Friedman Report: Maryland home prices held, but the clock didn't. Mortgage rates jumped 19 basis points to 6.95%, the fourth straight weekly increase, and the median time to go under contract stretched from 20 days to 25, the biggest weekly slowdown this report has tracked. The median sold price still rose 1.1% to $449,900. Homes sold for a median 98.4% of original list price, down from 99.6%, but 100.0% of final list price. The Friedman Market Momentum Index reads 44/100: Balanced Market, Cooling.

In this update: the full statewide numbers, a county-by-county supply breakdown showing where new listings outpace closings (Baltimore City and Wicomico at 3.2x, Frederick tightest at 1.4x), St. Mary's County as the fastest market in the state, Garrett County as the slowest, the 1.6-point gap between first ask and final price, and what it all means if you're buying or selling in Carroll, Baltimore, Howard, Frederick, Montgomery, or Prince George's County this fall.
