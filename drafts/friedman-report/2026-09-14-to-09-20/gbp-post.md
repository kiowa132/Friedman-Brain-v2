Prices Held. The Clock Didn't.

Denise and Walt listed their Frederick County home at $549,900. Their agent said $525,000. "We can always come down," Walt said. Then Freddie Mac put the rate at 6.95%, up 19 basis points in one week. By Friday they repriced to $529,900. By Sunday night they had an offer in hand.
