# Video Script: The Friedman Report, Week of September 14-20, 2026

Format: 15 to 20 second short (Reels / Shorts / TikTok). Talking head, all
live to camera. About 58 words. Read straight off the phone. Stat-first
order and "Look at the clock" opener are Kyle's picks (2026-09-20): the 25
vs 20 days leads, then the rate, then the price, then the one-line takeaway.

## The script

Look at the clock. The typical Maryland home took 25 days to go under
contract. Last week, 20.

Time is where sellers are losing leverage.

Mortgage rates jumped 19 basis points this week. 6.95%. Nearly seven.

And Maryland prices didn't flinch. The median sold price went up, to
$449,900.

So if you're selling, week one is your window.

## Where every number comes from

- 6.95%, +19 bps: Freddie Mac PMMS, released 9/17/2026 (was 6.76% the week
  before, 4th straight weekly increase, 6.26% a year ago)
- $449,900: statewide median sold price, 984 closings (last week $445,000, +1.1%)
- 25 days: median days to contract (closed CDOM), last week 20
