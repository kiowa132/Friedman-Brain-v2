# Video Script — Escalation Clauses & Appraisal Gaps

Talking-head, ~18-20 sec vertical (9:16). **Seller's-agent POV** — this is
an opinion / how-I-think piece, not a buyer tip. Kyle reads the lines
below; visual notes are what to show while saying each part.

## Post caption / hook line
"The highest offer on your house isn't always the one you take. Here's why
I read past the number."

---

## The script (read straight through — ~19 sec)

> "You get five offers on your house. The highest one's thirty grand over
> asking — escalation clause, appraisal gap covered. Looks like the
> winner. But if it appraises low, that buyer suddenly owes tens of
> thousands in cash they didn't plan for. And that's when deals fall
> apart — after you've already turned everyone else away. Knowing which
> offer actually makes it to closing? That's the job."

---

## Beat by beat — what to say / what to show

| Say this | Do / show this |
|---|---|
| "You get five offers on your house. The highest one's thirty grand over asking —" | Kyle at a table, a small stack of offer sheets in front of him, tapping the top one. Or straight to camera. |
| "escalation clause, appraisal gap covered. Looks like the winner." | Two quick text tags on the top sheet: **ESCALATION** / **GAP COVERED**. Small green check. |
| "But if it appraises low, that buyer suddenly owes tens of thousands in cash they didn't plan for." | The green check flips to a red flag. Number on screen: **APPRAISAL −$30K**. Kyle's expression shifts. |
| "And that's when deals fall apart — after you've already turned everyone else away." | The other offer sheets slide off the table / fade out. Beat of silence. |
| "Knowing which offer actually makes it to closing? That's the job." | Back to a clean shot of Kyle. End card: **The Friedman Team · Numbers Over Guesswork**. |

## Alternate opening lines (if you want a different hook)
- "Five offers on your house. I might tell you to pass on the biggest one."
- "The biggest number isn't always the best offer — and picking wrong costs you weeks."
- "Anyone can get you offers. The skill is knowing which one actually closes."

---

## 15-sec cut

> "Five offers on your house, and the highest is thirty grand over asking
> with an appraisal gap covered. But if it appraises low, that buyer's
> short tens of thousands in cash — and the deal breaks after you've
> turned everyone else away. Reading which offer actually closes is the
> job."

---

## Delivery notes
- Talk like you're leveling with a seller at their kitchen table, not
  presenting. Slightly lower energy than a buyer hype video.
- Real pause after "turned everyone else away" before the last line.
- The last line is the whole point — say it flat and certain, no upsell,
  no "call me."
