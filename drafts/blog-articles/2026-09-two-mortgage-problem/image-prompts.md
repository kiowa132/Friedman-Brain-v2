# Carousel image prompts — The Two-Mortgage Problem (photo-forward, attention-first)

5 slides for an Instagram / LinkedIn carousel driving to
`/blog/the-two-mortgage-problem-maryland-move-up-buyers`.

Design principle: **magazine cover, not a quote card.** Every slide is a
real, clearly-visible photograph joined to bold type by a gradient seam —
exactly like the friedmanreteam.com blog hero (visible house photo on one
side, solid teal type panel on the other). One big idea per slide, one
dominant number or word, and a reason to swipe.

**Run all 5 in one ChatGPT session.** Paste the "Shared template" block
first, then each slide prompt in order ("same template, slide 2 of 5").

## Shared template (paste this first, before slide 1)

> You are designing a 5-slide real estate carousel for **The Friedman Team**
> that must stop a scroll. Every slide follows the SAME template:
>
> - **Canvas:** 1080 × 1350 px, 4:5 vertical, full-bleed.
> - **Photo is mandatory and prominent on every slide.** Each slide is a
>   real, warm, editorial photograph — a real Maryland house, real people,
>   real daylight — occupying a clear zone of the frame (a full side, a
>   band, or a large inset). It must read as an actual photo, NOT faded to
>   a ghost or a faint outline. The photo and the type meet at a smooth
>   **gradient seam** in the brand teal (or cream): the type sits on the
>   solid-color zone, the photo is fully visible in its zone, and they
>   blend where they meet. Reference the friedmanreteam.com blog hero for
>   this exact split.
> - **Palette:** deep teal `#0D2226` / `#0F5C63`, warm gold `#C9A96A`,
>   cream `#FAF8F5`. No other colors outside the photography.
> - **Type:** headlines in a heavy high-contrast serif (Playfair Display /
>   Canela feel), set BIG — the main line fills most of the type zone,
>   tight leading, a few words per line. Kickers/labels in a clean sans,
>   ALL CAPS, wide tracking. Key numbers are blown up huge as a design
>   element.
> - **Layout:** ~80px margins on the type zone. Thin gold rule under the
>   kicker. Bottom-left: small "F" monogram in a thin gold circle +
>   "THE FRIEDMAN TEAM" caps. Bottom-right: page counter ("01 / 05") and,
>   on slides 1–4, a small gold `→`.
> - **Feel:** sharp, editorial, confident — not hypey. No stock clichés
>   (no handshakes, no houses on coins, no arrows-and-charts). No drop
>   shadows, no rainbow gradients. Photos lightly desaturated, warm.
> - Spell all text EXACTLY as given. No invented text on documents, signs,
>   or screens in the photos. Everything legible at phone size.

## Caption to pair with the carousel

Two houses, one closing window — that's where most move-up buyers in
Carroll, Howard, and Frederick County stall out. You've got the equity and
the motivation; what you don't have is a clean way to buy the next house
without being on the hook for two mortgages at once.

There are three ways around it:
→ Sell first and rent your home back for up to 59 days
→ Buy first by borrowing against the house you're leaving (2%–11%)
→ Write a contingent offer — the weakest hand in a tight market

Which one fits has more to do with your timeline and how much uncertainty
you can stomach than your bank account. Full breakdown — rent-backs, bridge
loans, HELOCs, 80-10-10s, and the Maryland forms that protect you when
you're buying and selling at once — on the blog. Link in bio.
