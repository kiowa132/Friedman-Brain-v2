## 5. How the buy-before-sell timing works

You can buy before Lewis Ln sells. For the roughly 30 to 60 days both homes are in play, the new payment plus Lewis Ln at about $1,700 runs about **$5,250 to $6,200 a month** for that short window.

Two ways to cover the down payment during the gap, and your lender picks the cheaper one:

- A **bridge loan or HELOC** against Lewis Ln before we list, paid off at settlement.
- Put **less down from savings** now, then **recast** the new loan after Lewis Ln closes. That is a one-time re-amortization, about $250, that resets the payment down to the $250K-down row above.

We list Lewis Ln the week you go under contract on the new home. A clean, updated Havre de Grace home in the low $400s should move in about 2 to 4 weeks.

## 7. Questions to work through before we look

Your answers set the real budget and the short list:

- **Garage:** must it be a true 3-bay attached garage, or would a 2-car plus a detached shop or extra parking pad work? This is the biggest single price lever.
- **Land:** how much yard? A normal lot under an acre, or 1 plus acre with privacy?
- **Town:** any preference or dealbreaker among Havre de Grace, Aberdeen, Bel Air, Churchville? Commutes for both of you?
- **New vs. resale:** new builds around $600K to $700K exist in Havre de Grace with a 3-car option. Open to that, or do you want mature trees and an established lot?
- **Guest room:** does it need to be a main-level bedroom or suite, or is any spare bedroom fine? All bedrooms upstairs, or a main-level primary?
- **Condition and timeline:** turnkey only, or would you take cosmetic updates for a better lot or price? When do you want to be in, and is there a school-year constraint?
- **Comfortable payment:** what is the top monthly number you are okay with? That is the real ceiling, not the pre-approval.
- **Down payment vs. cash kept back:** how much of the equity and savings goes down, how much stays liquid?
- **Rank your list:** footprint, garage, yard, open concept, guest room, square footage, beds, baths. Which three are non-negotiable?
