The short version:

We have not set a list price for Lewis Lane yet, so treat the proceeds as a wide
range for now. After the mortgage payoff and roughly 7 percent for sale costs,
your net proceeds should land somewhere around $200,000 to $325,000. We tighten

A home matching your list in Havre de Grace, Aberdeen, Bel Air and Churchville is
trading in the $650,000 to $760,000 range right now, based on what has actually
sold and what is active. About $825,000 is roughly the resale ceiling here, and
homes priced above about $850,000 are sitting. The 3-bay garage on real acreage
is the piece most likely to push you toward the $775,000 to $850,000 band or
toward new construction with a 3-car option.

With the Lewis Lane equity down, the new payment runs about $3,550 a month at a
$675,000 purchase to about $4,490 a month at $800,000. Your current payment is
around $1,700, so figure roughly $1,850 to $2,800 a month more, in exchange for
close to double the house.

You can buy before Lewis Lane sells, and the money from that sale does not have
to sit on the sidelines. Whatever you do not use for the down payment can be
applied straight to the new mortgage as a principal paydown once Lewis Lane
closes. Then you ask the lender to recast the loan, which is a one-time
re-amortization for a small fee, usually around $250. Your monthly payment drops
to match the lower balance while your rate and your term stay the same. It is not
a refinance, so there is no new loan, no new rate, and no closing costs.

A common way this plays out: you buy with a smaller down payment from savings so
you keep cash on hand for the move, then when Lewis Lane sells you put a big
chunk of the proceeds against the new loan and recast. For example, buying at
$750,000 with $200,000 down is about $4,430 a month; applying another $50,000
from the sale and recasting brings it to about $4,110, and applying $100,000
brings it to about $3,800. During the 30 to 60 day overlap before Lewis Lane
sells, we bridge the gap with a bridge loan or a HELOC against Lewis Lane,
whichever your lender prices cheaper.

A few things I am estimating and want to nail down with you on the call: a
realistic list price and condition for Lewis Lane, your exact loan balance and
current payment, and how much cash you want to put down versus keep liquid.
