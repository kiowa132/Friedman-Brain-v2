## 4. The number that matters

**At $750,000, with Lewis Ln equity down, the new payment is about $4,100/month —
roughly $2,400 a month more than he pays now.** At $675K it's about +$1,850/mo; at
$800K about +$2,800/mo. That jump is the trade for roughly double the house.

## 5. How the buy-before-sell timing works

He can buy before Lewis Ln sells. For the ~30–60 days both are in play:

- New PITI (~$3,550–$4,490) **+ Lewis Ln ~$1,700** ≈ **$5,250–$6,200/mo** for that
  short window.
- Fund the down payment in the gap one of two ways, lender picks the cheaper:
  1. **Bridge loan or HELOC against Lewis Ln** before listing — pays off at
     settlement.
  2. **Put less down from savings now, then recast** the new loan after Lewis Ln
     closes: a one-time re-amortization (~$250 lender fee) that resets the
     payment down to the "$250K down" row above.
- List Lewis Ln the week we're under contract on the new one. In this market a
  clean, updated Havre de Grace home in the low $400s should move in ~2–4 weeks.

## 7. Questions for Jose and his wife to work through before we look

Kyle doesn't have answers to these yet. Their answers set the real budget and the
short list:

- **Garage:** is a true 3-bay attached garage a must, or would a 2-car plus a
  detached shop / extra parking pad work? (Biggest single price lever.)
- **Land:** how much yard? A normal neighborhood lot under an acre, or 1+ acre
  with privacy? (Drives both price and which town.)
- **Town:** any preference or dealbreaker among Havre de Grace, Aberdeen, Bel
  Air, Churchville? Commutes for both of them?
- **New vs. resale:** there are new builds around $600K–$700K in Havre de Grace
  with a 3-car option. Open to that, or do they want mature trees and an
  established lot?
- **Guest room:** does it need to be a **main-level** bedroom/suite (parents,
  accessibility), or is any spare bedroom fine?
- **Layout:** two-story with all bedrooms up, or a main-level primary bedroom?
  How important is a finished basement / walk-out / second kitchen?
- **Condition:** turnkey only, or would they take cosmetic updates for a better
  lot or price?
- **Timeline:** when do they want to be in? Any school-year constraint for the
  kids?
- **Comfortable payment:** what's the top monthly number they're okay with? That
  sets the real ceiling, not the pre-approval.
- **Down payment vs. cash kept back:** how much of the equity + savings goes
  down, how much stays liquid?
- **Rank the list:** footprint, garage, yard, open concept, guest room, square
  footage, bed count, bath count — which three are non-negotiable?
