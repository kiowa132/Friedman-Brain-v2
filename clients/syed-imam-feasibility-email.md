HOW YOU LEGALLY RENT A BASEMENT IN-LAW SUITE

Assuming the lower level is already a finished, livable in-law suite with its own entrance, kitchen, and bath, there is no construction. You just need permission to place a tenant.

- Accessory-apartment or ADU registration, if the suite is not already on record as a legal second unit: $100 to $600
- County rental license plus the required inspection: about $60 plus a $100 to $200 inspection in Baltimore County, renewed every 3 years; $93.50 with inspection in Howard County, renewed every 2 years
- Maryland lead rental registration and inspection, for homes built before 1978: $300 to $500 up front, then $30 to $50 a year

All in, roughly $300 to $1,500 one time to be allowed to rent it, then about $100 every two to three years to renew. If the county requires a zoning hearing to recognize the suite as a legal second unit, add $1,000 to $3,000. If the suite adds a bedroom to a home on septic, the health department may want a capacity review at $250 to $750; a system upgrade, only if required, is separate.

HOW YOU LEGALLY RENT A DETACHED GARAGE AS AN ADU

Assuming the garage already has plumbing, air conditioning, and electric, the work is legalizing it as a rentable dwelling, not building it.

- ADU permit or registration: $200 to $1,500, by right in Howard County, possibly a hearing in Baltimore County
- Building permit and plan review to sign off code items such as an egress window, interconnected smoke and CO alarms, a compliant kitchen, and fire separation: $200 to $1,000, plus a $3,000 to $12,000 allowance only if any of those are missing
- County new-dwelling-unit fees, meaning the Howard County road excise and school surcharge, though ADUs may be exempt from the school portion under state law; Baltimore County is minimal: $0 to $6,000
- Septic review for the added unit: $250 to $750; upgrade only if required
- Rental license and lead registration, same as the basement option: a few hundred dollars

All in, roughly $1,000 to $8,000 in fees to legalize and license it, plus the code allowance only if the garage needs egress, alarms, or a kitchen finish to pass. Baltimore County caps a detached ADU at 800 square feet on lots under an acre and 1,200 over an acre. Howard County caps it at 75 percent of the main house.

Centennial High is a 10, Marriotts Ridge ranks number 12 in Maryland, Mount Hebron number 26, and the elementary and middle schools across the area generally rate 8 to 10.
