| Unit type | Gross rent | Net after vacancy, upkeep, license, higher insurance (~20–25% haircut) |
|---|---|---|
| Finished basement suite, 1 BR, separate entrance | $1,400–1,800 | **~$1,100–1,450/mo** |
| Finished basement suite, 2 BR | $1,700–2,100 | **~$1,350–1,700/mo** |
| Detached ADU / cottage, 800–1,200 sf, 1–2 BR | $1,900–2,700 | **~$1,500–2,150/mo** |

- Most Phoenix/Sparks homes are on **well + septic**. Septic capacity caps total bedrooms —
  adding a unit or bedrooms often forces a septic review and sometimes a system upgrade
  ($15K–40K). This is the #1 technical risk. Verify per property with the Baltimore County
  Health Dept before writing an offer.
- Short-term (Airbnb) could gross more but northern-county STR demand is thinner, and
  Baltimore County requires separate STR registration ($300/unit). Long-term is the base case.
- **Rental income from a unit that doesn't exist yet will NOT help Syed qualify for the
  mortgage.** Only an existing, leased unit with history counts, and only partially. Lender
  confirms. So he has to qualify on his own income for the full new payment.

**Net:** a detached ADU roughly covers the $750K payment gap; a basement suite covers about
half to two-thirds of it. At a $650K purchase, a basement suite can get him at or below
today's payment.

## 3. Baltimore County ADU / accessory-apartment rules (verify current status — in flux)

- **Maryland ADU Act of 2025 (SB 891 / HB 1466)** requires every county to allow **one ADU —
  attached or detached — rentable to anyone** on any single-family detached lot. County
  conforming ordinances are due **October 1, 2026**. As of Aug 2026 Baltimore County is
  mid-transition: its older "accessory apartment" statute was historically limited (family
  members, special-exception in many zones); a 2023 amendment broadened who could live there
  and removed the no-rent-collection rule. **Before Syed relies on renting an ADU to a
  non-family tenant, confirm the current adopted ordinance with Baltimore County Permits,
  Approvals & Inspections (PAI).**
- **Size caps (Baltimore County):** 800 sf on lots **under 1 acre**, 1,200 sf on lots **over
  1 acre**; also may not exceed **75% of the main dwelling** (state cap). Most Phoenix/Sparks
  lots are 1+ acre → 1,200 sf.
- **Permits:** building permit + plan review through PAI. On septic: Baltimore County Health
  Dept / MDE review, possible capacity upgrade. Well output may also be reviewed.
- **Rental license:** Baltimore County rental registration (County Code Title 35) — register,
  pay fee (~$48–60/unit for owner-occupied), pass a licensed inspection, **renew every 3
  years**. STR is a separate $300/unit registration.
- **Lead:** any pre-1978 home being rented (in whole or part) → Maryland MDE lead rental
  registration + risk-reduction inspection.

## 4. Cost to legally rent the unit (per Kyle: assume the space already exists)

**Assumptions locked with Kyle (3rd pass):** the basement is an **already-livable in-law
suite** (own entrance, kitchen, bath — no construction). The **detached garage already has
plumbing, A/C, and electric**. So this section is the *fees to legally place a tenant*, not
a build estimate. (The full itemized build breakdown is parked in
`clients/Syed-Imam-Cost-to-Add-a-Rental-Unit.pdf` / `scripts/pdf/adu-cost-breakdown-pdf.py`
if a "needs work" scenario ever comes up.)

**Basement in-law suite → legal rental:**
- Accessory-apartment / ADU registration if not already a legal 2nd unit: $100–600
- Rental license + inspection: Balt. Co. ~$60 + ~$150 insp. / 3 yrs · Howard $93.50 + insp. / 2 yrs
- Lead rental registration + inspection (pre-1978): $300–500 up front + $30–50/yr
- **Total ~$300–1,500 one-time**, ~$100 to renew. +$1,000–3,000 if a zoning hearing is needed.
- Septic capacity review if it adds a bedroom on septic: $250–750 (upgrade separate, only if required)

**Detached garage (already has plumbing/AC/electric) → legal ADU rental:**
- ADU permit / registration: $200–1,500 (by-right Howard; possible hearing Balt. Co.)
- Building permit + plan review to sign off code (egress window, smoke/CO, kitchen finish,
  separation): $200–1,000 + a $3,000–12,000 allowance *only if* those items are missing
- County new-dwelling-unit fees (Howard excise $1.91/sf + school $8.35/sf, ADU may be
  SB-543 exempt; Balt. Co. minimal): $0–6,000
- Septic review: $250–750
- Rental license + lead: a few hundred
- **Total ~$1,000–8,000 in fees**, + code allowance if the garage needs egress/alarms/kitchen.
