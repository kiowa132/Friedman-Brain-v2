# The Friedman Team Listing Roadmap (10 Steps)

Reusable for every listing presentation. Property-agnostic. Pairs with the Canva
10-circle timeline graphic. The card text below matches the original graphic's
style: short title, 3 to 4 terse bullets, no sentences. Talk track for delivery
is at the bottom.

**"WE ARE HERE" marker sits on Step 1 in every presentation.** Map pin above
circle 1 in brand teal or gold, a "WE ARE HERE" pill with a small "Today's
meeting" line, circle 1 filled solid while 2 through 10 stay outlined or light
gray, short arrow from 1 toward 2.

House style: no em dashes, no "no pressure," "fill out the listing paperwork"
not "sign the listing agreement."

---

## Card text (paste into Canva)

**1. PLAN**  *(WE ARE HERE)*
- Fill out listing paperwork
- Evaluate comps: active, pending, sold
- Price with the data, one number, hold it
- Net proceeds review

**2. PREPARE + PRESENT**
- Professional measurement + floor plan
- Staging, prep, disclosures
- Best-in-class photos, twilight, video
- 3D Matterport tour
- Zillow Showcase virtual staging

**3. COMING SOON**  *(Thursday)*
- Live on Zillow + MLS as Coming Soon
- Boosted Zillow search placement
- Paid Google + social ads start
- Track saves + tour requests

**4. GO ACTIVE**  *(the next Thursday)*
- Active, showings open
- Full syndication + agent email blast
- Property website + QR signage
- Neighborhood print

**5. OPEN HOUSE + BROKERS OPEN**  *(that weekend)*
- Public open house, first weekend
- Brokers open for agents with buyers
- Direct calls to agents with buyers

**6. FEEDBACK + ADJUST**
- Showing feedback within 48 hours
- Weekly call on activity + pricing
- One decisive move if needed, no slow bleed

**7. NEGOTIATE**
- Review every offer, price + terms
- Offer deadline when demand supports it
- Ratify contract, disclosures acknowledged

**8. UNDER CONTRACT**
- Manage inspection, appraisal, financing timelines
- Negotiate repairs or credits
- Transaction coordinator on every deadline

**9. CLEAR TO CLOSE**
- Financing clear-to-close
- Clear title
- Schedule settlement

**10. SETTLEMENT**
- Final walk-through
- Sign + fund
- Keys + proceeds to you

## Talk track (say this at the meeting, not on the graphic)

1. PLAN: "Think of this like the map at the mall. We are right here. We decide
   everything up front, because your first week on the market is most of the
   buyer traffic you will ever get and you only get it once."
2. PREPARE + PRESENT: "This is where the sale is won. We measure it right so it
   lands in the right searches, then the photos, the Matterport tour, and the
   Showcase staging get a buyer to spend time with the home and picture their
   family in it. Buyers decide with emotion."
3. COMING SOON: "Thursday is when buyers plan their weekend. We bank a week of
   interest before we go live, so we launch to a waiting list, not an empty
   room."
4. GO ACTIVE: "Homes that go active on Thursday go under contract about five
   days faster. Everyone who saved it gets it fresh right before weekend tours."
5. OPEN HOUSE: "We concentrate the demand into set windows so buyers are in the
   house at the same time. Seeing other buyers is what produces offers."
6. FEEDBACK: "Showings with no offers is data, not a dead end. We read it and
   act once, on purpose."
7. NEGOTIATE: "When there is more than one buyer, we set a deadline and let them
   compete for your home."
8. UNDER CONTRACT: "About 30% of deals nationally fall apart after the contract
   from poor coordination. That does not happen on our side."
9. CLEAR TO CLOSE: "We stay on it through the wire so nothing slips in the last
   two weeks."
10. SETTLEMENT: "You are done, and the number matches what we planned in step
    one."

## Supporting data (deck footnotes or conversation)

- Redfin: Thursday-listed homes go under contract about 5 days faster than
  Sunday. https://www.redfin.com/news/best-day-to-list-your-home-for-sale-2019/
- Zillow: ~250 views/day in week one puts a home on pace for contract within a
  week; 500+ tends to close at or above list.
  https://www.zillow.com/research/save-shares-views-35038/
- Zillow Showcase: 20% more likely to get an accepted offer within 14 days,
  ~2% higher sale price, 75% more views, 68% more saves, 75% more shares.
  https://www.zillowgroup.com/news/zillow-showcase-brings-listings-to-life/
